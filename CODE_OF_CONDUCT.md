# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Give and accept technical feedback professionally.
- Protect private information and report security concerns through the process in
  `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `OWNER_CONTACT`. Project maintainers will
review reports promptly and may remove content or restrict participation when
needed. Replace this contact before publishing the repository.
